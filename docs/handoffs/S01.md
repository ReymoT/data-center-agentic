## Calling interface and transition semantics

```python
from dc_sustainability.simulator import SchedulingSimulator

# Required development choice; neither value resolves Q01.
sim = SchedulingSimulator(allow_completion_starts=False)
visible = sim.reset(private_episode, forecasts_by_slot={})
visible = sim.state()
transition = sim.step(approved_actions)
# After exactly 30 steps:
result = sim.result(run_id="RUN-DEV", system="greedy_a")
```

`reset` accepts the existing PrivateEpisode, revalidates nested records and requires
24 decision slots, six completion slots, hourly timing, arrivals 0–17, deadlines
at most 30, consistent redundant job constraints and capacity evidence. Impossible
individual jobs are retained. Background above capacity is an invalid configuration.
Validation occurs before replacing an existing run. Reset discards that run's
history only after validation succeeds. No global RNG, wall clock or external I/O
is used.

At boundary t, `state()` shows arrived waiting jobs, running intervals occupying t,
completed intervals ending at or before t and explicit missed IDs. State version
is t. Waiting jobs become missed once t exceeds their last permissible start:
min(deadline, 30) minus duration, additionally capped at arrival for nonflexible
jobs and at 23 when completion starts are disabled. Already impossible arrivals
are marked at their arrival boundary, including boundary zero on reset. They are
never silently dropped. Missed jobs are excluded from waiting work and remain
visible as IDs. They remain unfinished in final results. This records physical
outcomes; it does not approve Q02/Q03 coverage or abstention semantics.

`step` revalidates ApprovedActions, episode/slot/version binding, uniqueness,
current starts, waiting-job membership, deadlines, nonflexibility and every
remaining slot's occupancy against authoritative IT capacity including background.
The batch is atomic: one invalid start rejects all new starts. Rejection still
processes existing work and advances the clock exactly once. Plan, dict, None,
stale actions and malformed model-copy objects cannot start jobs. Typed
ApprovedActions are a trusted runtime capability: their check ID/hash are not
cryptographic approval evidence. Matty's A04 must authenticate the passing check,
bind it to the plan, serialize latest-state checking/application and prevent
untrusted callers from constructing capabilities. S01 does not fabricate that gate.

Accepted jobs run at constant power over [start, start + duration), without moves,
pauses or cancellation. A duration-one job occupies its start slot and completes
at the next boundary. A SlotRecord for t records load and all jobs occupying t;
its completed IDs are those finishing at boundary t+1. The returned local immutable
Transition adds a rejection reason and new missed/nonflexible-violation IDs at
boundary t+1. `history` is an immutable tuple of these local diagnostics, not a
new shared schema. Initial impossible jobs appear in reset's visible missed IDs.
At t=30, state remains available and further step calls raise without mutation.
`result` requires all 30 slots and supplies the existing EpisodeResult, including
completed, missed and unfinished IDs. Missed/unfinished sets may overlap as F02
permits; completed IDs never overlap them. Energy, emissions and completed-work
normalization remain unset pending S02/P01 and Q03.

## Visibility, forecasts and completion-period choice

Public state uses VisibleState.from_snapshot to hide future-job constraint IDs.
It exposes no scenario, split, seed, actual-carbon observations or reference
answers. Missing forecasts do not alter private actuals or physical feasibility.
`forecasts_by_slot` is an optional explicit mapping from observation boundary to
existing CarbonForecast records. Each batch is revalidated against F02's horizon
and source-resolution rules. No observations are automatically relabeled as
forecasts, no issue times are invented, and withheld episodes reject supplied
forecasts. Missing batches mean unavailable forecast evidence, explicitly listed
in missing_carbon_slots. The caller must supply forecasts available at each
boundary under an agreed release rule. Q04 issue-time/freshness behavior is not
certified by this module. It has no episode UTC origin from which to infer it.

There is no default completion-start policy. With False, starts end at 23 and
24–29 drain running work. With True, current approved starts may execute through
29 if they can finish by deadline and boundary 30. This is development scaffolding
for both alternatives, not accepted research settings. Neither path stores future
plan commitments or calls a model. If Q01 adopts committed future starts, A04 and
all affected consumers must agree on approval/recheck/cancellation semantics.
