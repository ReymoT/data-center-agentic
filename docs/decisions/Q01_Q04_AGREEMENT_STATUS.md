- Q01: new starts through slot 23 only; slots 24-29 finish work, ending at boundary 30. Future starts are intentions rechecked against current state before execution.
- Q02: per-job infeasibility does not stop feasible work. Missing forecasts differ from physical infeasibility. Joint contention between waiting jobs and Q08 objective/certification details remain unresolved.
- Q03, Nisha/Ved/Matty confirmations: cover every waiting job exactly once with a start, deferral or justified abstention. No new starts of running/completed/missed/unknown jobs. Deferral needs a later legal start through 23 meeting deadline, without guessing future capacity. Nonflexible jobs and last-start jobs cannot simply defer. Whole-decision abstention needs independent justification for every waiting job. Missing/stale carbon alone is not physical infeasibility.
- Accuracy: required missing/malformed/blocked outputs are incorrect. Primary denominator is each system's slots with waiting jobs; secondary all 24 slots; report empty counts. Separate initial/repaired/fallback/executed stages and LLM-originated versus final validity. Record actual system-specific denominators/queue counts/state hashes: states can diverge.
- Empty queue: deterministic no-op with zero model calls for both systems. This remains a pending shared implementation requirement. L01 currently has no guard, and runtime/E02 are not implemented. Matty owns runtime boundary work, Nisha L01/E02 integration. Verify zero dispatch/attempts and saved no-op records before claiming it works. Ceilings 6480/2160 remain maximums.
- Completed work: count jobs and IT energy before PUE by job ID. Unfinished/missed work earns no completion credit. Savings/exact-gap claims need identical completed-job sets, horizon and inputs; otherwise record not comparable and count it.
- Q04: issue time differs from observation time; age at most two slots fresh, older stale, future issue times invalid. Forecast calculations require full fresh coverage. Unusable forecasts lead B/C/D to decision-wide checked A, with unsupported impact claims withheld. This is not a physical-infeasibility verdict.

A03 implements fixed IDs/timing/deadlines/nonflexible starts and all running/planned occupancy, including future intentions. It still returns unresolved-rule errors otherwise; A04/runtime and full checker acceptance are not complete. No shared default, schema, solver choice or production check changed here.
