Caption: this is the rules-v1 candidate/fallback path. The common runner also checks Greedy A/B proposals; it does not route every failed baseline check through another Greedy A attempt. Greedy B can choose Greedy A internally when its forecast is unusable. The production checker gates execution and is not Nisha's independent oracle. The runner rereads state/snapshot both before and after the recheck; approval applies only to current starts. Selected empty plans can pass as no-ops. Failed approval drains; out-of-scope rules input or changed live state instead stops without stepping. Records/hashes detect changes but do not authenticate their own source. The independent path establishes no execution permission. Safe refusal is not P1 success when it prevents completion of eligible feasible work. Main's historical 176361a archive is received evidence, not verification of corrected main.
