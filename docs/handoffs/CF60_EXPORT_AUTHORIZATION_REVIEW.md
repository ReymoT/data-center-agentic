## Limits and stop conditions

Both routes: at most 128 MiB aggregate JSON input, 32 MiB export output and 900
seconds total work, with separate at-most-three-second process cleanup. The
supervisor deducts preparation time and never releases a late worker. Windows
process creation itself is not interruptible; there is no portable fallback.
Capture has at most 1200 controller transitions and 1200 pre-step snapshots;
recovery has zero transitions. Both have zero retries, CSV opens, downloads,
model/solver calls, label-cache writes, classifier inference, fitting or policy
study execution. Do not print private values or paths.

Changed code/input, bad scope, missing evidence, reused ID, existing output,
timeout, crash, leaked process, invalid/missing state or exceeded cap stops the
operation. Keep partial artifacts and failure/dispatch receipts; do not resume or
accept apparently complete output from a failed dispatch. Each attempt consumes
its ID. No automatic fallback from recovery to capture or from one source to
another is permitted.
